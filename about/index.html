About ShiftScope AI

ShiftScope AI is built by an emergency medicine resident physician.

New AI tools for clinicians launch every month, but most reviews are written for clinic visits, not for the ED. Emergency clinicians need to know different things: will it survive constant interruptions, keep six open encounters straight, ignore the hallway noise, and actually work in Epic ASAP, FirstNet or MEDITECH?

ShiftScope AI tracks 42 tools today, with sourced facts for every listing. Physician verdicts, written by emergency clinicians against ED-specific criteria, are coming soon.
