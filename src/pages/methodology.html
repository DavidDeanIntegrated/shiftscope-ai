How we review

ShiftScope AI judges tools against how an emergency department shift actually works, not against a generic clinic visit.

Two layers: verified facts and physician verdicts

  1. Verified facts (live now). For each tool we record the company, category, key features, EHR integrations, public pricing, HIPAA/BAA status, FDA status, published studies and the source URL for every claim, plus the date we last checked. If we couldn't find something in a public source, we label it Unknown or Not publicly stated. We never guess.
  2. Physician verdicts (coming soon). An emergency physician uses the tool (on a real account, outside of patient care or under their institution's approval) and scores it on the ED criteria below. Until that happens, every listing says Physician review coming soon and shows no rating.

Physician review process

  • Reviewers are practicing emergency clinicians (attendings, residents and APPs). Each review names the reviewer's role and training level.
  • Reviewers disclose any financial relationship with the vendor or its competitors. Anyone with a conflict doesn't review that tool.
  • Where we can, a second EM clinician reads each verdict before it's published.
  • Reviews are dated and get re-checked when a vendor ships a major change.
  • Vendors may send factual corrections, and we fix verified errors quickly. Vendors cannot edit, approve or preview verdicts.
  • No PHI is used in reviews unless the reviewer's institution has approved the tool and a BAA is in place.

Conflict-of-interest & ad policy

  • Sponsors never affect verdicts, ratings or rankings. Paying us cannot buy a better score, remove criticism, or get a review published faster.
  • Sponsored placements are always labeled "Sponsored" and kept visually separate from editorial content.
  • Featured listings (if we offer them) are labeled "Featured" and change only position and visibility, never the facts or verdict.
  • Affiliate and referral links are disclosed next to the link. Some vendors (for example, Heidi) run public referral programs. If we use one, we'll say so, and it never changes the verdict.
  • The default sort is never pay-to-rank.
  • We publish a list of current sponsors on the Sponsor page.

What we are not

We aren't a clinical guideline, a procurement recommendation, or a substitute for your institution's IT security, privacy and clinical governance review. See the disclaimer.
