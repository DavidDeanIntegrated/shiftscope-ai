Vscan Air with Lung Guidance

by GE HealthCare · vscanair-support.gehealthcare.com ↗

AI acquisition guidance on the Vscan Air handheld that walks users through a standard 8-zone lung ultrasound protocol.

Physician review coming soon

No rating has been assigned. We don't publish scores until an EM physician has evaluated the tool against our ED criteria.

Key features (as described by vendor and sources)

  • Detects lung landmarks to guide the user to a reference point in each of 8 zones (upper/lower anterior, upper/lower lateral, both sides)
  • Auto-captures 5-second clips per zone with an optional chime
  • Does not detect or annotate pathology; images must be interpreted by a qualified physician
  • Indicated for patients older than 21; a paid add-on in the Vscan Air app, not available in Guest mode
