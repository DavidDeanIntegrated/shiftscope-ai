Published evidence

  • 2026 review in npj Digital Medicine noting the limited published evidence on OpenEvidence
  • Clinician Use of an AI-Enabled Clinical Information Platform (JAMA, Oct 15, 2026; usage study of 271M queries from 952,008 NPI-verified US clinicians; data supplied by OpenEvidence and 3 of 7 authors list OpenEvidence affiliations; measures use, not accuracy or outcomes)

Notes

Per the Oct 2026 JAMA usage study, 88% of emergency physicians' queries were about topics outside emergency medicine (the highest of any specialty). On Oct 15, 2026 OpenEvidence, Doximity and Pathway Medical told a federal court they had reached a settlement in principle in their lawsuit; terms were not disclosed.
