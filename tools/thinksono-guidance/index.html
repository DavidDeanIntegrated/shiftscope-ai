ThinkSono Guidance

by ThinkSono · thinksono.com ↗

AI guidance that walks non-ultrasound-trained staff through a lower-extremity DVT compression scan on a handheld Clarius probe, for remote review.

Physician review coming soon

No rating has been assigned. We don't publish scores until an EM physician has evaluated the tool against our ED criteria.

Key features (as described by vendor and sources)

  • Real-time probe-positioning and compression guidance for a standardized lower-extremity DVT exam
  • Records compression cine-loops and sends them to a dashboard for remote review by a qualified clinician
  • Does not interpret images (acquisition guidance only, per FDA indications)
  • Works with compatible Clarius handheld scanners

Published evidence

  • Vendor-reported multicenter study (1,691 patients, 24 sites): non-experts got diagnostic-quality images in 87.1% of exams; 92.9% sensitivity, 97.1% specificity (as summarized by HLTH; full paper not yet linked)

Notes

Interesting for after-hours EDs without vascular ultrasound: the scan can be done by trained non-sonographers and read remotely. US commercial availability was still being prepared as of the July 2026 announcement.
