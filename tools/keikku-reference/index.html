Keikku Reference

by Lapsi Health (Keikku) · keikku.health ↗

Cited clinical Q&A that now includes ACEP clinical policies, with a free tier and an optional ambient scribe that feeds patient context into answers.

Key features (as described by vendor and sources)

  • ACEP clinical policies integrated into Reference under a clinical and educational collaboration announced Nov 12, 2026 (no commercial terms; ACEP says it is not an endorsement)
  • Free plan: unlimited Reference with citations from PubMed, WHO and NIH, plus 10 Scribe sessions a month with copy-paste into any EHR (per vendor pricing page)
  • Clinician Pro ($89/mo billed annually): EHR integration, context-aware Reference Plus, more sources (NICE, CDC, FDA, local guidelines) and ICD-10/CPT/HCC coding (per vendor)
  • Reference can draw on the patient record (via EHR integration), prior sessions, the live Scribe transcript and incoming labs and imaging (per vendor)
  • Runs on the BODHI framework, meant to state uncertainty and ask for missing information rather than over-answer (per vendor)
