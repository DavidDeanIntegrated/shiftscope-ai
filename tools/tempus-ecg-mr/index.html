Tempus ECG-MR

by Tempus AI · tempus.com ↗

12-lead ECG algorithm that flags signs of undiagnosed moderate or severe mitral regurgitation in at-risk adults 65 and older.

Physician verdict

Physician review coming soon

No rating has been assigned. We don't publish scores until an EM physician has evaluated the tool against our ED criteria.

Key features (as described by vendor and sources)

  • Analyzes resting, non-ambulatory 12-lead ECGs recorded at a healthcare facility and returns a binary output
  • Intended for patients 65+ with cardiovascular risk factors or symptoms such as dyspnea, chest pain or lower extremity edema, without prior mitral valve intervention or known moderate/severe MR
  • Not a standalone diagnostic; not for serial monitoring or paced rhythms
  • Vendor-reported performance: PPV 13.9% and NPV 97.8% at the 6.0% MR prevalence seen in its clinical study
