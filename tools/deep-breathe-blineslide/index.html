BlineSlide

by Deep Breathe · deepbreathe.ai ↗

Lung ultrasound AI that detects B lines and, as of an Oct 2026 clearance, absent lung sliding (a pneumothorax warning sign).

Key features (as described by vendor and sources)

  • Absent lung sliding detection (510(k) K261679, Oct 23, 2026): concurrent reading aid that flags absent pleural line motion (lung sliding or lung pulse) on lung ultrasound cines in adults 18+; vendor calls it the first FDA-cleared AI dedicated to this finding
  • Per the FDA summary: standalone sensitivity 0.94 and specificity 0.87 on 818 cines; in a 25-reader study, aided reader sensitivity rose from 0.716 to 0.880 and AUC from 0.855 to 0.922, while the specificity gain (0.820 to 0.841) was not statistically significant
  • B line artifact module: detects presence or absence of B lines in lung ultrasound clips (K252007, Oct 2025; standalone sensitivity 0.91, specificity 0.84 on 1,005 cines per the FDA summary)
  • Cloud service: you upload de-identified MP4 cines (non-linear probes only, 2.6 to 7.8 seconds, 18+ fps) within 1 hour of acquisition; it returns 'Cannot Evaluate' for clips that don't qualify. Validated on Sonosite, Philips, Butterfly, Mindray and GE systems
  • Vendor says its software works with any ultrasound system and runs on-device on iOS and Android

Notes

Per the FDA summary, care that's clinically indicated (including intervention for suspected tension physiology) shouldn't wait for a BlineSlide result, and patient management decisions shouldn't rest on its output alone. Absent sliding alone doesn't diagnose pneumothorax. In the reader study, emergency physicians' aided specificity (0.88) was about the same as unaided (0.89).
