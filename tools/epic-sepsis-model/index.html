Published evidence

  • Wong et al., External Validation of a Widely Implemented Proprietary Sepsis Prediction Model in Hospitalized Patients (JAMA Intern Med, 2021): hospitalization-level AUC 0.63
  • Evaluation of a Commercial Pediatric Sepsis Early Detection Alert in the Emergency Department (JAMA Pediatrics, 2026): 166,943 pediatric ED encounters at 8 New York sites; 36% sensitivity for Phoenix-criteria sepsis within 24 h (30% before onset), PPV 3%, NPV >99%; lower sensitivity in Black children (22%), children under 5 and non-tertiary EDs. Epic disputes the comparison criteria
