Ceribell with Clarity

by CeriBell, Inc. · ceribell.com ↗

Point-of-care EEG with FDA-cleared AI that marks possible electrographic seizures at the bedside, including in the ED.

Physician verdict

Physician review coming soon

No rating has been assigned. We don't publish scores until an EM physician has evaluated the tool against our ED criteria.

Key features (as described by vendor and sources)

  • Single-use EEG headband (or infant headcap) plus a pocket recorder that records, stores and displays EEG in visual and audible formats in a professional healthcare facility (FDA indications)
  • Clarity seizure-detection software marks previously acquired EEG sections that may correspond to electrographic seizures and notifies when detected seizure prevalence is Frequent, Abundant or Continuous (ACNS Guideline 14)
  • Age coverage across separate 510(k)s: infants from 25-44 weeks postmenstrual age and under 1 year (K252070); patients 1 year and older (K241589); the original recorder software described adult (>=18) detection (K191301)
  • Status Epilepticus Monitor (K223504) is indicated as an aid for diagnosing electrographic status epilepticus in adults >=18 at risk for seizure, in acute care, used with other clinical information; not a substitute for EEG review of other patterns and not for epilepsy monitoring units
  • EEG can be streamed to Ceribell's cloud portal for remote review (per vendor). A separate Delirium Monitor (K251936) is cleared only to aid screening and monitoring of delirium in adult critical-care patients, not as a standalone diagnosis

Published evidence

  • Wright et al., Evaluating the utility of Rapid Response EEG in emergency care (Emerg Med J, 2021): 38-patient prospective 2-ED pilot. Physicians reported a change in management for 20/38 (53%) and expedited disposition for 8/38 (21%). Partially funded by Ceribell; one author is a Ceribell scientific advisor.

Notes

FDA labeling also warns that the recorder is not defibrillation-proof and is MR unsafe (remove before defibrillation or MRI). Delirium monitoring is cleared for adult critical-care settings, not specifically the ED. Existing stroke and ICH imaging tools (Viz.ai, RapidAI, Brainomix, etc.) were left in Imaging triage.
