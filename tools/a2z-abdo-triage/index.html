a2z-Abdo-Triage

by a2z Radiology AI · a2zradiology.ai ↗

Abdomen-pelvis CT triage that flags suspected acute findings such as appendicitis, obstructing stones and solid-organ trauma for prioritized reading.

Key features (as described by vendor and sources)

  • Flags suspected positives on abdominopelvic CT, with or without IV contrast, in adults 22 and older
  • Sept 2026 clearance covers 10 findings: obstructing ureteral stone, hydroureter, retroperitoneal hematoma, large-volume ascites, acute appendicitis, abscess, hemoperitoneum or complex free fluid, and splenic, hepatic and renal traumatic injury
  • Company says its suite now covers 17 abdominal findings across two FDA-cleared devices
  • Works alongside the radiologist's read; does not replace interpretation
